## Source and usage notes

These are unaltered gameplay images opened and visually checked from the official Press Kit linked by the [Satisfactory Press & Content Creators page](https://www.satisfactorygame.com/press/). Individual original files are linked below. The official page directs press members and content creators to the kit, but the page reviewed here does not state a blanket commercial-reuse or editing license. This log records the source and intended context without claiming a broader license.

Every added figure says whether it shows the article's exact calculation or only general gameplay context. Images with the older Update 1.1 HUD are labeled as historical reference material, not as a current UI walkthrough. Assets are kept unaltered. Alt text describes visible content; captions distinguish the image from a solved plan or demonstrated build.
