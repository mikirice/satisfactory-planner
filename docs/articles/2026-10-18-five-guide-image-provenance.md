# Five guide image sources (2026-10-18)

All game screenshots below were opened and visually checked in the official Coffee Stain Studios Satisfactory Press Kit linked from the [Press & Content Creators page](https://www.satisfactorygame.com/press/). The page points creators to the public kit but does not state a blanket commercial-blog license, crop/overlay policy, or required credit format. Captions identify each screenshot's source and limits; this record does not claim broader permission for unrelated media.

The covers use the established GameRoamer 1280×720 template and contain editorial titles, orange labels, and a dark text field. No in-game UI was presented as the cover typography. The Depot and Project Assembly covers reuse one official factory image as broad context; each caption states its limited use. Covers are locale-specific and the same file is wired to the article hero, listing card, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.

Body figures are separately described at point of use. The Throughput Monitor body image shows the Press Kit's 14, 24, 195, and 200/min readings, which belong to that pictured scene and are not article measurements. The Priority Merger body image shows the complete settings panel (Left Low, Center and Right High). The Project Assembly figure is an authored calculation diagram, not a game screenshot. The Hypertube figure is an Update 1.1 Press Kit screenshot and is explicitly not represented as current-client behavior. The Depot guide has no diagnostic screenshot.
